Status 14 Nov 2026: everything below is built and live, except face expressions on the scanned models (no facial rig) and nicknames (dropped). Schedule and rituals are made up and easy to change in `SCHEDULE` and `RITUALS`.

Approved ideas. Everything stays inside the office scene: no rooms or events outside it.

## Decisions and interaction
1. **Ask for help.** When a deadline is close, the person walks to the colleague they trust most, says what they are stuck on (their real goal of the day), and both look at the screen until it is solved. It leaves a memory and a stronger bond.
2. **"Not now" and coming back.** A busy person can decline a visit with their real reason ("I need X done by 3:15"), and later walks over to the other person to keep that promise.
3. **Coffee invite.** Before a coffee run they ask a desk neighbour. The neighbour says yes or no for their own reasons (tired, deadline, just had one) and walks along if yes.
4. **Chiming in.** Someone sitting near a conversation overhears it and joins with a line at the end.

## Realism
5. **Coat and bag.** People arrive with a jacket and bag, hang the jacket and leave the bag under the desk, and do the reverse when they leave. On rainy days they carry an umbrella.
6. **Listening.** The listener nods, looks at the speaker's face and sometimes laughs.
7. **The cup empties.** Coffee goes down sip by sip, and an empty cup sends them to refill it. They wait if the coffee machine is busy.
8. **Printer.** Someone prints, walks to pick up the sheet, and now and then it jams.
9. **Tiredness through the day.** Quick and upright early, more stretching, yawning and rubbing eyes around 4 pm.
10. **Short breaks.** Standing back stretches, looking out the window for a while.
11. **Evening light.** At dusk the screen lights their face and someone turns on the desk lamp.
